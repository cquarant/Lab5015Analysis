Form a DUT–REF coincidence

Step 1 reads the reconstructed events at 3 V. Each pass chooses one bar of DM0348 and maps its L and R sides to the configured REF ASIC channels. Both sides must have a hit in the same event entry. A DUT bar can then contribute its own L and R measurements from that entry. The bars on the two modules can have different numbers.

Each REF selection runs independently. An event with several active REF bars can pass more than one selection. See how repeated observations are counted.

Require a REF signal

An active REF bar has valid hits at both ends, both times over threshold (ToTs) between −10 and 100 ns, and positive mean raw energy. Events with more than three active REF bars are rejected.

For the chosen REF bar, step 1 first applies the TOFHIR energy factors for each end (3 V, REF T1 = 10). At each DUT threshold it fits the calibrated mean REF energy with a Landau function. The accepted interval starts at 0.70 times the fitted location, with a 20 ADC fallback when the fitted location is at most 20 ADC. Its upper limit is 950 ADC.

Select DUT bars and write the event tree

The DUT uses TOFHIR-only energy factors for its bar, end, overvoltage and threshold. Its multiplicity veto accepts up to three active bars. The count requires positive energy at both ends and both ToTs between −10 and 100 ns. For the bar stored in the tree, both ToTs must be strictly between −10 and 50 ns, and each calibrated threshold-zero setting plus the scanned threshold must be at most 63.

The tree stores the DUT and selected REF timestamps, energies, ToTs and fine clock phases. The published REF7 sample used an older active-bar count. The current selection checks both REF channel indices before reading the corresponding hits. Use “REF7 · same selection” for comparisons with the multi-REF result.

From selected events to timing residuals

Reference characterization trains the REF time-walk corrections in coincidence with fixed DUT bar 7. Step 2 then trains the DUT energy and clock-phase corrections for each selected REF. Timing replay requires all four raw timing residuals within 5,000 ps, four ToTs in [0, 25] ns and mean DUT energy within the saved energy interval. A group needs valid corrections and at least 100 accepted coincidences.
