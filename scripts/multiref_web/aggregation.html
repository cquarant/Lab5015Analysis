One entry per accepted coincidence

We combine the samples of REF bars 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 after applying the time corrections trained for each REF. Comparisons are made separately for every run, DUT bar and T1 threshold. REF6 and the peripheral REF bars were excluded based on detector performance.

The union contains 18,496,110 DUT–REF coincidences from 16,783,116 distinct DUT-bar observations. Of these, 1,459,113 pass several REF selections. Each coincidence contributes to the union. Shared observations are identified by both raw DUT timestamps within the group, with matching DUT energies and phases.

Align the zero of each REF sample

For each of the five timing residuals, compute the mean in each REF sample and the mean over all coincidences. Subtract the sample mean and add the pooled mean. Then pool the shifted residuals and extract the four resolutions. This is done separately after time-walk correction and after the final phase correction.

Shifting a sample preserves its internal width. Before alignment, the spread between REF means also contributes to the pooled width. Across the 384 run–bar–threshold groups, the median span of the mean DUT-average-minus-REF residual is 847 ps.
